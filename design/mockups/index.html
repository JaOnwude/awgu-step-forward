01 — BRAND

A mark about the next step

Three rising steps toward a morning sun: progress you can see, taken one verified step at a time. The steps also read as the hills of Awgu. It is built from simple rectangles, so it stays crisp at 16 px and costs almost nothing to load.

AWGUStepForward
AWGUStepForward

Lockup

“AWGU” is set in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, tracked +22%, in laterite. “StepForward” is set in Fraunces SemiBold. Keep the mark’s height equal to the two lines of the wordmark, with clear space of half a step on every side.

Voice

Plain, warm and direct. Say what is verified and what is not. Never promise placement, funding or jobs. Write for a first-time phone user on 2G: short sentences, one action per screen.

Do not

Do not recolour the steps, add gradients or shadows to the mark, use the mark as a government seal, or place it on busy photos. The wordmark is never set in another typeface.

Why these typefaces

Fraunces is a soft, warm serif with real character. It makes headlines feel editorial and human rather than like a government portal.

Plus Jakarta Sans has open shapes and a tall x-height, so forms, prices and dates stay readable at small sizes on low-end phones.

Both are free (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted through next/font, subset to Latin, and limited to four weights in total, about 90 KB.

Space & shape

4-point scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. Radii: 8 inputs-sm · 12 inputs · 16 cards · 24 panels · full for buttons and chips. Touch targets are at least 44 px (48 dp on Android).

Trust & safety

How verification works

  1. Providers send proof of who they are and a reachable contact.
  2. A staff member checks every new listing before it is public.
  3. Changes to pay, deadline or eligibility go back for review.
  4. Anyone can report a listing; urgent safety reports are reviewed the same day.

Verification means our team reviewed the provider. It is not a government endorsement or a guaranteed place.
